Model Risk and RAG Monitoring Policy

The RAG assistant must provide source-cited responses for policy, compliance, and operational questions.
Hallucination monitoring is performed weekly using a validated ground-truth dataset and monthly using human review.
Any production release with a faithfulness score below 0.85 must be blocked until remediation is approved by the model risk owner.

Prompt experiments are tracked in MLflow with prompt id, model name, retriever settings, latency, and evaluation metrics.
