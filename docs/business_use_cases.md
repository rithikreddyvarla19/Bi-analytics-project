# Business Use Cases

## Executive reporting
- Daily sales, regional performance, and top product/category trends.

## Revenue operations
- Repeat purchase rate and LTV proxy segmentation.
- Payment success vs failure trend monitoring.

## Customer experience
- Clickstream funnel proxy from page view to purchase.
- Channel-level engagement and sessions.

## Supply chain
- Inventory stockout rates by region and day.
- Reorder pressure monitoring using reorder points.

## Returns management
- Return rate and refund trends by product and channel.
- High-return products for quality intervention.
