# Business Insights Summary

## Executive Snapshot

- Total recognized revenue reached **$8.36M** across **11,169 orders**.
- Average order value is **$748.53**.
- Latest month-over-month growth is **10.23%**.
- Repeat purchase rate is **73.47%**, showing strong customer recurrence.
- Overall refund rate is **4.00%**, with manageable post-sale leakage.

## What the Data Indicates

- Revenue concentration is strongest in **Sports** and **Fashion**, which are the top categories by revenue contribution.
- Regional performance is led by **Southeast**, **Southwest**, and **West** in current output.
- Monthly demand shows spikes in **March**, **July**, and **December**, suggesting campaign or seasonality effects.
- A high repeat rate plus stable refund levels implies customer satisfaction is generally healthy, but return-risk monitoring remains important in high-volume segments.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize inventory and promo investment in top categories (Sports, Fashion) while protecting margins.
2. Replicate top-performing regional playbooks from Southeast/Southwest into weaker regions with localized pricing and assortment tests.
3. Launch retention journeys for first-time customers within 30 days to raise the repeat rate further.
4. Target high-refund SKUs for quality checks, content improvements, and sizing/expectation clarity.
5. Track cohort retention and category growth monthly in the executive dashboard to detect trend breaks early.
